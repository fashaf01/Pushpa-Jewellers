Suba Mangala

A gold purchase plan over 6 or 12 months

Own exquisite 22KT gold through easy monthly instalments. Choose an amount from Rs 1,000 to Rs 10,000 a month, save for 6 or 12 months, and Pushpa adds a cash gift at the end.

  • At the end of your 6 or 12 months, choose jewellery for the full amount.
  • Pay your instalment on time each month.
  • If you stop early, you can still buy jewellery for what you have paid, without the cash gift.
  • Payments are not refunded in cash.

    The pride of Negombo

    Pushpa Jewellers opened on Greens Road, Negombo, in 1967. Today we have three showrooms in Negombo and Katunayake, and we still run them the way we began.

    In Negombo a jeweller is someone you know. For almost sixty years, families have come to us to choose a wedding necklace, to mark a birth, to put gold away for the future, and sometimes just to ask a question. We try to deserve that trust one piece at a time, with honest gold, the weight of every piece shown, and a price we are happy to explain.
