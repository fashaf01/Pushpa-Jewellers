Own exquisite 22KT gold through easy monthly instalments. Pay a fixed amount each month, and Pushpa adds a cash gift at the end.

  • At the end of your 6 or 12 months, choose jewellery for the full amount.

  • If you stop early, you can still buy jewellery for what you have paid, without the cash gift.
  • Payments are not refunded in cash.
