Since 1967

Negombo's trusted goldsmiths for three generations

Pushpa Jewellers opened on Greens Road in 1967, and the flagship showroom has stood on the same street ever since. Families who bought their wedding jewellery from us now bring their daughters.

That trust rests on plain things: gold you can have independently assayed, prices tied openly to the day's rate, and staff who will talk you out of a piece that isn't right for you.
