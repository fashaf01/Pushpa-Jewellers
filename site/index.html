      Know your gold

      Karats, the pawn and how gold is priced. Everything we explain at the counter, in one place.

      Purity

      What the karat tells you

      A karat is a share of twenty-four. The higher the number, the more of the piece is pure gold.

      24KT

      Pure gold, 999 parts in a thousand. Too soft for most jewellery, so it is kept as coins and bars.

      99.9%
      22KT

      916 parts in a thousand. The traditional choice for Sri Lankan jewellery: deep in colour and strong enough to wear. Most of our pieces are 22KT.

      91.6%
      18KT

      750 parts in a thousand. Harder and paler, which holds small stones well in fine pieces.

      75.0%

      The pawn

      Eight grams, the Sri Lankan way

      In Sri Lanka gold is counted in pawn. One pawn is eight grams, and the day’s rate is quoted per pawn. We print every piece’s weight in grams, so use this to see it in pawn and how much pure gold it holds.

      • 1 pawn = 8 grams
      • Half a pawn = 4 grams
      • A 24.380 g necklace is 3.05 pawn

      Care

      Keep it for the next generation

      Store pieces apart

      Keep each piece in its own pouch, so stones and chains do not scratch each other.

      Take it off for water

      Remove gold before swimming, bathing or cleaning. Chlorine and soaps dull it.

      Perfume first

      Put on perfume and lotion before your jewellery, and let them dry.

      Clean it gently

      Warm water, a drop of mild soap and a soft cloth. Dry it well before you put it away.

      Questions
      Asked at the counter
      
      
      
        
      Do you show the weight of every piece?
      Yes. Every piece we show carries its weight in grams. We price gold by weight at the day’s rate, so you always know what you are paying for.
      
        
      What is a pawn?
      Eight grams of gold. Many families in Sri Lanka plan gold in pawn, so we show weights in both. The gold guide has a converter.
      
        
      Which karats do you sell?
      Most of our pieces are 22KT. We also make pieces in 18KT, which holds small stones well.
      
        
      Can you make a piece to my design?
      Yes. Bring a sketch, a photograph or an old piece and we will talk it through at the counter.
      
        
      Do you offer gold pawning?
      Yes. Bring the gold and your national identity card, and we will explain the day’s rate and terms.
      
        
      Do I need an appointment?
      No. Walk in whenever we are open. For bridal sets, booking ahead means we can set aside time for your family.
