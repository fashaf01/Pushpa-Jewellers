About Pushpa Jewellers

A Negombo family of jewellers since 1967. Three showrooms today, and one way of working: honest gold, the weight of every piece shown, and a price we are happy to explain.

1967

Where it began

Pushpa Jewellers opened on Greens Road, Negombo, in 1967. Greens Road is still our head office, and the home of Pushpa.

In Negombo a jeweller is someone you know. For almost sixty years, families have come to us to choose a wedding necklace, to mark a birth, to put gold away for the future, and sometimes just to ask a question.

Today there are three Pushpa showrooms, on Greens Road and Sea Street in Negombo and at Averiwatte in Katunayake, and we still run them the way we began.

How we work

Trust, one piece at a time

Almost sixty years on, these are still the things we hold to at every counter.

  1. Honest gold

    22KT and 18KT gold, carrying the 916 and 750 marks.

  2. Weighed in front of you

    Every piece is weighed while you watch, and its weight is shown in grams and in pawn.

  3. Priced at the day’s rate

    Gold is priced at the day’s rate, and we are happy to explain every price.

  4. People you know

    The people who help you choose your gold are the same people you pawn with, change money with and book your flights with.
